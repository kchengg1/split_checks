Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Short version: Settled collects nothing. Everything you put into the app — receipt photos, items, people, groups, and expenses — stays on your iPhone, unless you choose to share a group, which uses your own iCloud. There are no accounts with us, no servers of ours, no analytics, and no third parties.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system and no backend. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, and we have no way to see your data.

Your data stays on your device

All content you create — bills, itemized receipts, the names of people you split with, groups, and expenses — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage, and deleting the app removes it. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you deliberately share something, as described next.

If you choose to share a group

Two features send data, and only when you ask for them:

  • Sharing a copy as a file. The group is written to a file that you send however you like — AirDrop, Messages, anything else. It goes wherever you send it; we are not involved.
  • Sharing a group live through iCloud. That group is stored in your own iCloud account and shared with the people you invite, using Apple's iCloud sharing. It is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not ours — we operate no server and cannot see it. The people you invite can see and edit that group's expenses, payments, and member names. Stop sharing at any time from the group's menu.

Groups you never share stay on your device. Receipt photos attached to an expense are stored on your device and are not included in a shared group.

Camera and photos

If you scan a receipt, the camera (or a photo you choose) is read entirely on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. Receipt images are used only to extract the line items and are never sent to us or anyone else.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Nothing you do in the app is monitored.

Sharing a summary

When you tap Share to send a split or settle-up summary, iOS's standard share sheet lets you choose where it goes (for example, a Messages thread). That action is initiated and controlled entirely by you; the app does not send anything on its own.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
